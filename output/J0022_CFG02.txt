D. Additional comments. Include required S/N requirements for typical targets in your field.

We have 2 nights assigned for this project. 
Overall Goal: 2 hr integration in each of 6 fields (2 per each of 3 regions)
The targets in this setup: Lya emitters at z~3.2, selected trough narrow band imaging. We do not expect continuum but we do expect to see Lya at the center of the spectrum.

We want two sky fibers per 16 fiber block (a total of 32 sky fibers), ideally at the top and bottom of each block.

We have also added 16 V>~17.5 stars to use as calibrations. In the catalog files they have priority=7 and have their Vmag indicated in the last column. We would like these near the center of each fiber block if possible to avoid contaminating the sky spectra.

In case weather does not cooperate, we would prefer to secure at least 2 hours in one mask per region.

This field is best observed around the middle of the night.
